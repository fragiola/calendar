The time grid's layer of columns is the grid's last row and each column its day's gridcell, so each day's list of timed events sits in a cell (axe reports no violation). A day's "+N more" link is the last item of its events on the keyboard: Down from the last event (or Enter on a day whose events are all hidden) focuses it, Up goes back, Escape returns to the day.
