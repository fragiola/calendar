Start `@fragiola/calendar-vue`: the `Calendar.*` primitives for the month, time-grid and list views in Vue idioms (`as` and the `#render` scoped slot, scoped slots for every collection, template refs), `v-model:events`, `v-model:view` and `v-model:date` with `@events-change`, `@view-change` and `@date-change`, the engine's and the features' events as listeners (`@select`, `@event-drop`, …), the composables, `typedCalendar<T, F>()`, `createCalendarRef` / `useCalendarRef`, and every feature's entry with its parts. A server render is the first client render.
